WHO WE ARE

About SafeCity

SafeCity is an academic frontend project that demonstrates how a digital crime reporting platform can organize incident information and provide users with a clear reporting experience.

The platform includes interfaces for submitting crime reports, sharing anonymous tips, tracking report progress and viewing report-related information through a user dashboard.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission & Vision

Using technology to demonstrate a more organized and accessible approach to incident reporting.

Our Mission

To provide a simple and structured digital interface where users can submit incident information, share anonymous tips and follow the progress of their reports.

Our Vision

To demonstrate how digital tools can support community awareness, organized information management and a more transparent reporting experience.
